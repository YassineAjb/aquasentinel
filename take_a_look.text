AquaSentinel — team brief (Kizuna 2026)

Idea
AI-assisted field monitoring: pick a farm site on a map, pull satellite imagery, compute NDVI / vegetation stats, compare dates, and flag areas that need inspection. Goal = help farmers use less water and less pump energy by acting only where the crop is stressed.

Why it fits Kizuna (water × energy × food)
- Agriculture: see field condition over time
- Water: irrigate / intervene only where needed
- Energy: fewer wasted pump cycles when the grid is weak
Core product is satellite + software. IoT (Pi / solar) is optional stretch only — not required for the demo.

MVP (must ship in ~34h)
1. Create a site + draw/upload field boundary
2. Fetch Sentinel-2 imagery (cloud filter)
3. Show map + NDVI + mean/min/max + date/source
4. Compare two dates (Δ NDVI)
5. Simple “inspect” flag + short evidence-based summary
6. One-page monitoring report

Skip for now
Full IoT sensors, solar power demo, drought diagnosis claims, heavy auth, big AI claims without evidence.

Pitch one-liner
“See crop stress from space early — so Tunisia’s farms waste less water and less energy when the grid can’t keep the pumps running.”
